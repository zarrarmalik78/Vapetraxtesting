# Shop Branding & Receipt Customization

## Overview
Shop Branding & Receipt Customization allows store owners to configure store headers, address, contact phone numbers, tax rates, logo graphics, and custom footer messages on thermal POS receipts.

---

## ⚙️ Document Target

Stored in [[Settings Collection]] (`/settings/branding`):
- `shopName`: Retail store title.
- `shopAddress` & `shopPhone`: Contact header.
- `showTaxDetails` & `taxRate`: Tax calculations.
- `footerMessage`: E.g., "Thank you for visiting VapeTrax! No returns without original receipt."
