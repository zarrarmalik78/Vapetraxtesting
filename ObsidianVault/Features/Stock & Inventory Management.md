# Stock & Inventory Management

## Overview
The Stock & Inventory Management feature enables full control over product catalogs, stock levels, low-stock warnings, purchase cost prices, and e-liquid bottle tracking.

---

## ⚙️ Core Functionality

1. **Product Management**: Add, edit, or archive items across categories (devices, coils, pods, e-liquids, accessories).
2. **Low Stock Alerts**: Automatically highlights items falling below `minStockLevel`.
3. **Bottle Unsealing**: Track open vs closed e-liquid bottles for partial refill sales.
4. **Bulk Management**: Protected bulk deletion and mass stock adjustments.
5. **Supplier Purchases**: Log incoming inventory shipments linked to suppliers.
