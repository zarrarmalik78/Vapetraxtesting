# Exportable & Printable Detailed Reports

## Overview
The Detailed Reports feature generates formatted financial statements, sales registers, inventory valuations, and expense logs formatted for printing or Excel export.

---

## ⚙️ Features

1. **Custom Date Filters**: Today, Yesterday, This Week, This Month, or Custom Date Ranges.
2. **Excel Export**: Uses sheet generators to download structured `.xlsx` workbooks.
3. **Printable Layouts**: Dedicated print styles for clean thermal or A4 reporting.
