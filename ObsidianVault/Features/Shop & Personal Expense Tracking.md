# Shop & Personal Expense Tracking

## Overview
VapeTrax provides strict dual-scope expense tracking to separate operational store expenses (rent, electricity, salaries, tea/coffee) from personal owner expenses (personal budget, home bills).

---

## ⚙️ Operational vs Personal Scopes

1. **Shop Expenses**: Recorded on [[Expenses Page]]. Deducted directly from Gross Profit to determine Shop Net Profit.
2. **Personal Expenses**: Recorded on [[Personal Expenses Page]]. Completely isolated from shop P&L statements to prevent accounting distortions.
