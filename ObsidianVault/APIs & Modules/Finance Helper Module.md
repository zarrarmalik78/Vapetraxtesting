# Finance Helper Module

## Overview
- **File**: `src/lib/finance.ts`
- **Scope**: Financial Metrics & COGS Calculations

---

## 🎯 Functions & Logic
- `calculateCOGS()`: Computes total cost of goods sold.
- `calculateGrossProfit()`: Revenue minus COGS.
- `calculateNetProfit()`: Gross profit minus shop expenses.
- `calculateMargin()`: Profit percentage calculation.
